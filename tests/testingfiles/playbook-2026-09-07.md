Market Expectations for September: Well, if there’s anything to be sad about the month of August, it’s that it was great for selling premium. Recent positive and negative headlines have had minimal overall impact on equity/bond prices as the conflict in the middle east has fallen into the background as a “forever war" in some circles. Markets don’t like uncertainty, volatility spikes when either the highest or lowest something can go is unknown, once it’s known the market adjusts. The market is comfortable with the level of disruption, it’s comfortable with the level of military interactions, the level of weaponry used. Economically, there is impending crash or boom, growth remains moderate. Bottom line: the market requires another catalyst (other than the positive bias provided via buybacks and inflation) to break out of the current range. Mid-term elections are 2 months away, and no supermajority on either side is projected. Trump could lose the House and be open to another round of impeachments, but history shows us that they all spectacle and zero substance. Trump isn’t going anywhere prior to Jan 20th, 2029. The data center narrative is played out. All that said, “never sell a dull market”. Expect both the intermediate and macro uptrends to remain intact for equities and yields to hold at current rates. International markets and metals could continue to see more “chop” on the intermediate term but even their macro trends seem not at risk. Yep, it is what it is til it isn’t. So, where does that leave us? Following the naked put sales of either the Trader Trend or Investor Trend are always a must. The question is how high is up and more importantly how fast is up? A slow methodical move up into the end of the year supports the conditional Bull Call spreads of the Investor Trend suggestions, but we need explosive price potential for the Trader Trend Bull Call Spreads to pay off. We don’t see anything “explosive” happening. Moving on to Commodities: Keep accumulating TLT via selling quarterly puts, 20 yr highs on the 30yr is a generational gift. Precious metals: the neutral bullish stance remains, expect large swings to continue, go wide and hide (surviving the Trader Trend suggestions is a daily coinflip) Crude oil: The highs are in. Swings will continue based on last headline, but the “shock” price when this first began is unlikely to be retested, more production is coming online, the “disruption” is being managed. In summary, expect little to occur the rest of the summer, Q3 might bring us some action… and we’ll deal with it then.
